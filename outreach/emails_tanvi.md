- Send each email on its own from your own mailbox: one recipient, no BCC, no bulk merge. Fill in `[LAST NAME]` in the signature and never edit the opt-out line (it is legally required for University of Sydney and UTS).
- At most 10-15 new emails a day, Tuesday to Thursday, in the recipient's morning. Sydney/UTS means their morning, which is the US afternoon or evening the day before.

- Where it says "no public email", use the contact form or lab page given, or skip the person. Never guess an address.
- Wave 1 "variant" emails: use one only if Nik sends you a deep link and you've spot-checked that extraction yourself. Otherwise send the normal first email.
- Log every send and reply in the prospects sheet `status` column (date and what happened).
- Forward any "yes" or protein/target reply to the team chat the same day. Remove anyone who says "no" or opts out immediately, and never contact them again.
