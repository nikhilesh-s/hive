# HiveBlot impact push: 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-20

Branch: `outreach/oct20`. The prospect sheet is [`prospects.csv`](prospects.csv): 43
unique schools, 1-2 people per school, every row with public Western blot evidence and a
source URL. Nothing here has been sent. Every email goes out by hand, from a real mailbox,
after a person reads it.

## The bet

A cold email that says "try our search engine" to a researcher whose protein is not in a
3-paper corpus gets ignored, or worse, gets tried once and returns nothing. The email that
gets answered says: **"we extracted the westerns from your paper; tell us what we got
wrong."** Researchers correct their own work. That one move does three jobs at once:

1. **Reply rate.** The hook is their own paper, not our product.
2. **Evaluation data.** Every author correction is an adjudicated label on an unseen paper.
   That is the missing piece behind "no accuracy number for unseen papers" (B-10, D-7), and
   it is the most defensible impact number we could have by 10-20.
3. **Corpus growth where it matters.** The live tier fills with papers from labs that
   actually run westerns, instead of whatever we happened to pick.

The concierge fallback, for people who do not want to review their own paper: "reply with a
protein and cell line and I'll send back every published western we can find, with
antibodies and dilutions." We do that part by hand. It does not scale; it does not need to.

## Pre-extraction ("your paper", Week 1 Mon-Tue)

For each prospect scored 70+:

1. Pick one recent open-access paper of theirs with westerns (the `wb_evidence_url` column
   is the starting point; a core-facility person can be skipped here).
2. Run live extraction locally with `claude_cli` and `WBM_CONCURRENCY=4`, loading into the
   hosted live tier (`hive_ingest`). About 4.5 min per paper; 40 papers is an afternoon.
3. **Tanvi spot-checks every extracted paper before its link goes in an email**: target,
   antibody, lane readout on at least two panels. A visibly wrong extraction of someone's
   own paper is worse than no email. If it is wrong, fix the prompt path or drop the hook
   and use the concierge template instead.
4. Record the deep link in the sheet (`deep_link` column, added at send time).

Papers without an open-access PDF fall back to the concierge template. Never scrape a
paywalled paper.

## Email templates

Plain text. Replace every `{field}`; if a field cannot be filled truthfully, use a
different template. Never claim familiarity that is not there.

### A. Author of an extracted paper (early-career PI, lab manager, postdoc)

Subject: `westerns in your {topic} paper`

> Dr. {last_name},
>
> We pulled the Western blots out of your {year} {journal} paper on {topic} and turned each
> one into a structured record: target, phospho-site, antibody and dilution, cell line, and
> what each lane shows, with the sentence from the paper behind every field:
> {deep_link}
>
> We're a small student team building HiveBlot, a search engine for published westerns, so
> people can check whether an antibody or setup has actually worked before they order it.
> Your paper is a good test because you know the right answers.
>
> Would you, or whoever ran those blots, spend five minutes telling us what we got wrong?
> Just reply to this email with anything that's off.
>
> {sender_name}
> {sender_affiliation}
> (If this isn't useful, reply "no" and I won't write again.)

### B. Core facility director or manager

Subject: `saturated bands`

> Hi {first_name},
>
> Your core runs {instrument} for a lot of labs, so you probably see westerns quantified off
> saturated exposures more than anyone. We built a free band-reading tool that reports
> ratios as ranges and refuses to give a number when the band core is clipped, instead of
> handing back a confident value: {link}
>
> We're students trying to find out whether that refusal is useful in practice or just
> annoying. Would you try it on one image from your own instrument and tell us which?
>
> {signature}

Touch 2 for this group asks the D-7 question: would one of their user labs run a two-fold
dilution series we can use to check the numbers.

### C. Faculty teaching a lab course with westerns

Subject: `western blot pre-lab`

> Prof. {last_name},
>
> I saw that {course} has students running westerns. We built a free tool that shows, for
> any target, how it has been blotted in published papers (antibody, dilution, cell line,
> lane readout, each backed by a quote from the paper), plus a band-reading tool that
> refuses to quantify saturated bands.
>
> It might work as a 20-minute pre-lab: students look up their target before they run it,
> then check whether their own image is quantifiable. Would that be useful for a section
> this term? {link}
>
> {signature}

### D. Researcher who works on blot rigor, antibody validation or image integrity

Subject: `your {topic} work`

> Dr. {last_name},
>
> {personal_hook} It's a big part of why HiveBlot refuses to report a band ratio when the
> band is clipped, keeps conflicting sources unresolved instead of picking one, and shows
> the quote behind every field it extracts from a published western.
>
> You would find the weak spots faster than anyone. Would you be willing to try to break it
> for ten minutes? {link}
>
> {signature}

### Follow-ups (every template)

- **Touch 2, 4 business days later:** new angle, not "checking in". "If it's easier: reply
  with a protein and cell line you're working on, and I'll send back every published western
  we can find for it, with the antibodies and dilutions used."
- **Touch 3, a week after that, last one:** "Last note from me. If the timing's wrong, no
  problem. If someone in your lab runs a lot of westerns, I'd be grateful for a forward."
- Stop after three. An opt-out or a "no" is removed the same day and never contacted again.

### Signature and compliance

Real name, role, school, a reply-able address, and a mailing address if HiveBlot is sent as
a company. Honest subject lines. Opt-outs honored the same day (CAN-SPAM allows 10 business
days; do not use it). Sydney and UTS fall under Australia's Spam Act: consent is inferred
only because the work address is conspicuously published and the email concerns their role,
so the sender identification and the opt-out line are mandatory there. Keep the source URL
for every address (it is in the sheet).

## What to measure by 10-20

Logged in the sheet (`status`, `replied_on`, `outcome` columns) and in `hiveblot_feedback`
via `scripts/export_feedback.py`.

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Personalized emails sent | 80 |
| Replies (any) | 20 |
| People who used the product (feedback rows or a session) | 12 |
| Papers with author corrections or confirmations | 8 |
| Field-level author-adjudicated labels | 150 |
| Design partners (a lab using it weekly, a course module, or a core recommending it) | 3 |
| Dilution-series commitment (D-7) | 1 |
| Quotable, attributable reactions (with permission) | 3 |

The readout on 10-20 is the first accuracy table on papers HiveBlot had not seen, graded by
their own authors. Report it per field with n, only where n supports it, and never as one
headline number.

## Rules that do not move

Reviewed corpus stays reviewed. Live rows stay `LIVE · UNREVIEWED` until an author or
Tanvi adjudicates them. No accuracy claim for unseen papers until the adjudicated table
exists. Never send a prospect a link to an extraction nobody has checked.
