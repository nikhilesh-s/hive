# HiveBlot cold emails: Yashvi's 42 prospects

## How to send

- Send each email by hand from your own mailbox, one recipient per email. No BCC, no bulk mail merge, no tracking pixels, no attachments.
- Send 10-15 a day at most, Tuesday to Thursday, 8-10 am in the recipient's time zone. If two people are at the same school (for example Taabazuing and Burslem at Penn, who share a department), send them on different days.
- Go in order: wave 1 first (Sep 29 to Oct 1), then wave 2 (Oct 6-8), then wave 3 (Oct 13-15). Follow-ups go as replies in the same thread, on the dates given, and stop after follow-up 2.
- Read every email before you send it and fix anything that isn't true. Replace `[LAST NAME]` in the signature. If a detail about their paper looks wrong to you, cut it rather than guess.
- Use a "Variant if Nik pre-extracted their paper" email only when Nik gives you a deep link that Tanvi has spot-checked. Otherwise send the first email.
- Log every send and every reply in the `status` column of the prospects sheet on the same day (for example `sent 09-29`, `fu1 10-05`, `replied 10-06`).
- Forward any "yes", or any reply that names a target, to the team chat the same day. Concierge answers should go back within 24 hours.
- Anyone who says "no" or asks to stop: mark them `opted out` in the sheet right away and never email them again.

Links in every email use `?lab={lab_tag}`. Keep that tag exactly as written. It is a neutral cohort tag.
