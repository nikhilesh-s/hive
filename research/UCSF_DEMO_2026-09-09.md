Branch `experimental/ucsf-demo-2026-09-09`. Local stack only (no hosted
experimental service). Everything below was rehearsed on this machine on
2026-09-09; the frozen beta (`feature/bio-context-beta`, hosted) is the
trust anchor and is untouched.

`api/.env` (gitignored) must carry `DB_READONLY_URL`, `DB_FEEDBACK_URL`,
`DB_INGEST_URL` (local pgserver DSNs from `scripts/local_db.py status`),
`WBM_LLM_BACKEND=claude_cli`, `WBM_MODEL=sonnet`, `WBM_CONCURRENCY=4`,
`UNPAYWALL_EMAIL=<your address>`, and `VALENCY_API_TOKEN=<token>` once
Srushti's token arrives (discovery falls back to PubMed Central without it).

## The story, in order (8–10 minutes)

1. **Trust anchor (reviewed).** `/search` → `P-ERK` → two Conflicting cards,
   "Why conflicting", both claims, no winner. Then `phospho STAT3 Tyr705` →
   open **Evidence & sources**: crop, quoted caption, lanes, DOI. Say: *three
   reviewed papers, every value traceable, and it refuses to guess.*
2. **Research page.** `/experimental/research`. Status line shows
   `model: claude_cli`, discovery source, live tier row count.
3. **Search a topic.** Type `autophagy LC3B western blot lung cancer` (or
   click the example). Discovery list appears with the source chip
   (**PubMed Central**, or **Valency** + PMC once the token is set) and an
   **open-access PDF · PMCxxxxx** chip per paper.
4. **Choose a paper with an open-access PDF.** Papers without one show
   "No open-access PDF" and cannot be chosen: *we only read what we are
   allowed to download.*
5. **Extract.** Click **Extract this paper live**. The stepper walks
   fetching → preprocessing → extracting → loading with the model named.
   Narrate the stages. Real timing on this machine with `WBM_CONCURRENCY=4`:
   about 1 minute per 4 panels; a figure-heavy paper (30 candidate panels) is
   5–10 minutes. **Do not wait on stage.** Say so and move to step 6.
6. **Pre-warmed paper.** Open **"Already extracted in this stack"** and click
   **Open extracted evidence** on one of the two pre-warmed papers (below).
   Cards render with **LIVE · UNREVIEWED** badges.
7. **Provenance.** Click **Evidence & sources** on a live card: crop from the
   live PDF, per-field sources, statuses, lanes. Say: *same engine as the
   reviewed corpus, no human review, that badge is the only difference.*
8. **Ask.** In "3. Ask about this paper", ask e.g. *"Which targets were
   blotted, in which cells, and is any phosphorylation claim unresolved?"*
   The answer cites `[E#]`; each citation is a link to the numbered evidence
   block below, live blocks are marked unreviewed, unresolved fields stay
   unresolved. If the model call fails, the numbered evidence still renders
   with a note (deterministic fallback).
9. **Experimental densitometry** (promised to the researchers; ~2 minutes,
   updated 2026-09-15 for v0.2). `/experimental/densitometry` → record id
   **4455** → Load crop (the STAT3 panel, two strips × three rows).
   a. Drag a box around the **P-STAT3 (Ser727)** strip of panel C only
      (roughly x 793–1402, y 120–185 in image pixels; the box readout under
      the image shows the numbers). Lane count **6** → six dividers appear;
      drag one if it sits on a band. Measure. Expected: green card *Valid
      within-image relative measurement*, ranking stable, each lane a
      **range** (e.g. `0.6–0.7`), geometry overlaid on the strip. Say:
      *pixel darkness, one image, no units, not protein amount.*
   b. Box the **β-actin** strip of panel C (y ≈ 335–400), 6 lanes, Measure.
      Expected: amber *Warning* — two lanes are a near tie for strongest,
      their order is not resolved, the other lanes keep their ranges (all
      0.8–1.0: even loading, as a loading control should be). Say: *it
      tells you what the image cannot decide.* (The T-Stat3 strip, y ≈
      227–292, reads valid with every lane at 0.85–1.0.)
   c. Refusal: record id **4686** (ACTB, BEX2 paper, `page_007_cand_0045`),
      Load crop, box the bottom ACTB strip over the blot only (x ≈ 672–1229,
      y ≈ 778–842; leave out the printed "ACTB" label), lane count 6,
      Measure. Expected: red *Quantification
      refused — the strongest lane is clipped*, no numbers, clipped % per
      lane (23–68%). Say: *refusing is the feature; most published bands are
      clipped.* (Fallback: upload any blot image with a solid-black band.)
   d. Export JSON shows method name, version, every threshold, region, lane
      bounds, image sha256 — enough to reproduce. Nothing is stored.
   Do not say "calibrated", "protein amount", "loading-control normalized"
   or compare two images. Validation on file:
   `research/experiments/D2_clip_threshold_study.md`,
   `research/experiments/D5_real_crops_refusal.md`.

## Fallback path

| Failure | What you see | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Model call slow/failed during live extraction | stepper stuck at "extracting" or "Extraction failed" | open a pre-warmed paper (step 6); the story continues unchanged |
| Chosen paper has no downloadable PDF | job fails at "fetching" with the open-access message | pick a paper with the **open-access PDF** chip; say why |
| Valency down / token missing | source chip shows PubMed Central only | nothing to do; discovery still works |
| `/ask` model failure | answer box shows the note; numbered evidence still listed | read the evidence blocks aloud; that IS the deterministic result |
| Web or API down | demo_check FAIL lines | restart from "Start the stack"; the frozen hosted beta at https://hiveblot-beta.vercel.app still shows the reviewed story |

## What to say and not say

- Say: reviewed corpus = 3 papers / 93 experiments / 475 rows, human-reviewed.
- Say: live extraction = same pipeline, no human review, badged, separate tier.
- Say: paper QA answers only from shown records and cites them.
- Do not quote extraction accuracy. The only number that exists is a
  self-consistency check on a paper we already reviewed (L1, see
  `research/experiments/L1_live_ingest_eval.md`): on the lanes that matched,
  protein identity agreed 100%, modification and experiment type ~83%,
  band presence ~86%, antibody catalog ~83%; lane label matching itself is
  imperfect. It is not unseen-paper accuracy.
- Do not present the 59.7% densitometry sanity check as anything (retired;
  header on `research/experiments/D1_densitometry_vs_bands.md`).
- Densitometry thresholds are v0.2 defaults justified on synthetic fixtures;
  a real dilution series (ticket D-7) is the next validation step. Say so if asked.
