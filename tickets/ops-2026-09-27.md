## O-2 · Most open-access papers still cannot be downloaded (blocks: concierge replies) — Nik (API) + Srushti (web)
Live check on the 18 wave-1 prospect papers after PR #5: 7 resolve (PLOS,
Nature family, microPublication). The rest fail honestly:
- 403 bot check on the publisher page: MDPI, PNAS, Science/Sci Adv, OUP (NAR,
  NAR Cancer), Wiley (FEBS Open Bio, Clin Transl Med).
- No `citation_pdf_url` tag: Elsevier (RPTH), EMBO Press (Mol Syst Biol).
- Europe PMC `?pdf=render` answers 403 to every user agent; OpenAlex lists no
  `pdf_url` for any of them.

Do not work around bot checks. The fix is a **bring-your-own-PDF path**: on
`/experimental/research`, a signed-in beta user (behind the access gate) can
upload a PDF for a DOI they name; the API checks `%PDF`, caps size (50 MB),
stores it where `fetch_pdf` would have, and runs the same job. The UI states
that uploads must be open access or the researcher's own paper. This unblocks
the concierge loop: Tanvi or Yashvi opens the OA PDF in a browser, downloads
it, uploads it. Acceptance: upload → job → records in the live tier with
`source = "upload"` in the run metadata; a non-PDF is a 400; tests for both.

Second option, only if uploads are not enough: Europe PMC `fullTextXML` plus
figure images (no PDF). Check first whether the figure image URLs are also 403.

## O-8 · The research page gives up on a running job after ~10 s of errors — Srushti (small)
During two concurrent hosted extractions on the free Render instance, every experimental
route answered **429** for about a minute (Render's edge, not our limiter: uvicorn never saw
the requests; search and history were unaffected). `web/app/experimental/research/page.tsx`
marks the job "Lost track of this job: backend returned 429" after 5 failed polls (10 s),
while the job keeps running server-side. Treat 429/502/503 as transient for at least 90 s
(show "reconnecting…"), and only 404 as final. O-1 (Starter) makes the spikes rarer.
